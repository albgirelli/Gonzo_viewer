The Gonzo dataset: CSF tracer in the human brain over 72 hours

Dataset & viewerData downloads

The Gonzo dataset follows an MRI contrast agent injected into the cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) of a healthy human volunteer for three days, as it spreads around and into the brain and is then cleared. It is made for data-integrated simulations of brain fluid transport.

Subject
One healthy volunteer
Tracer
0.25 mmol gadobutrol, intrathecal (lumbar) injection
MRI sessions
Before injection, then about 4, 24, 48 and 70 h after
Measurements
T₁ maps → tracer concentration (0.5 mm), T1w, T2w, FLAIR, DTI, blood plasma samples
Derived data
FreeSurfer segmentations, surfaces and a finite element mesh

Tracer concentration over time

Concentration estimated from T₁ maps, shown here at 1 mm (downsampled from 0.5 mm). Click anywhere in the images to see the concentration over time at that point. For research use, download the original data from Zenodo.

The Show menu also has the diffusion tensor imaging (DTI) maps, measured before the injection in brain tissue: how fast water diffuses (mean diffusivity, MD), how strongly it prefers one direction (fractional anisotropy, FA, from 0 = the same in all directions to 1 = along a single line), and the preferred direction itself, coloured red for left–right, green for front–back and blue for up–down, with brightness given by FA. Bright, coloured tracts are white-matter fibre bundles.
